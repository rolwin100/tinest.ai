# Image Prompts — gotiny.ai

Shared style string to append to every prompt for consistency:
`editorial, warm natural light, muted warm palette, no text, clean minimal background, soft depth of field, high-end photography`


## Use Cases — Scene Images
Format: Landscape 4:3 or 16:9

### Support lines
A professional in a minimal modern office space on a support call, calm focused expression, wearing a headset, large window with warm diffused light, clean beige desk, editorial workspace photography, warm neutral tones, shallow depth of field

### Audio content
A high-quality condenser microphone on a minimal desk, warm studio lamp casting soft light, small notebook and coffee cup nearby, editorial still-life photography, warm amber and cream tones, clean background, no text or logos

### In-product voice
A hand holding a modern smartphone showing a clean audio waveform interface, shot from above on a warm sand-colored desk, soft natural side lighting, editorial product photography style, muted warm tones, minimal props

### Dubbing & localisation
An open notebook with handwritten text in multiple scripts side by side, a pen, minimal warm desk surface, soft top-down editorial photography, warm cream and taupe tones, clean composition, no recognisable language dominant

## Hero Section — Atmospheric Feature Image
Format: Wide 16:9

Abstract sound wave or audio frequency visualization, photographed or rendered, warm backlighting diffused through mist, deep warm blacks in foreground fading to soft warm highlights, minimal centerline composition, no people, no text, cinematic editorial art photography, grain texture, warm monochrome
